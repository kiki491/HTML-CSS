Background


Founded in 2012 by three friends—an electrician, a plumber, and a carpenter—MultiCraft Home Services was created to solve a common headache: tracking down separate contractors for home repairs. Starting out in a shared garage with just one service van, they combined their trades into a single, seamless solution. Built on honest pricing, strict safety, and exceptional craftsmanship, the company has grown from a local startup into a premier fleet of certified multi-trade specialists delivering total property care under one roof.


Our Mission


Our goal is simple: to make home and building care effortless, dependable, and affordable.

We take the hassle out of property care by bringing certified expertise across plumbing, carpentry, and electrical trades straight to your door.

Whether you need a urgent pipe repair, custom woodwork, or an electrical upgrade, our team delivers high-quality workmanship, transparent pricing, and prompt solutions tailored to your needs.

We treat every property with the care and respect it deserves, ensuring safety, efficiency, and long-lasting results on every job.
